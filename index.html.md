## Projects

### Inside Glattbrugg

A web deep-dive into Glattbrugg, the place I grew up. There's more to it than the flight path.

A static site documenting my hometown: the spots that matter, the people, and what living next to an airport actually feels like. Built to practice layout, typography and storytelling on the web.

Tags: HTML, CSS, JavaScript

### Screen Time Reality Check

Turns your weekly screen time into uncomfortably honest numbers.

Enter your weekly screen time and it tells you what that adds up to over a year: books unread, runs not run and hours of sleep lost. A small tool with a big guilt trip, built in vanilla JS.

Tags: JavaScript, CSS

### Gmail Digest Bot

An automated summarizer that turns inbox chaos into one clean daily digest.

An automated email summarizer built with Claude Cowork that reads through my inbox and generates a clean digest, so I stop drowning in email. n8n-based automation planned as the next upgrade.

Tags: Claude Cowork, Gmail, Automation

### Prayer Times Widget

An iOS home-screen widget showing today's prayer times for Zurich.

Written in JavaScript with Scriptable. Fetches prayer times for Zurich, caches them for offline use, and highlights the next prayer with a countdown, so you see it at a glance right on your home screen.

Tags: Scriptable, JavaScript, iOS
